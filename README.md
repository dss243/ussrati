## 📜 Laws & Legislation

**Family Law in Algeria – Key Articles:**

- **Articles 4-10: Marriage Conditions**  
  Requirements for a valid marriage: consent, eligibility, guardian, witnesses.

- **Articles 48-58: Divorce**  
  Types of divorce, procedures, and effects on spouses and children.

- **Articles 62-74: Custody**  
  Custodian requirements, children's rights, custody duration.

- **Articles 75-83: Alimony**  
  Obligations, calculation methods, and claim procedures.
